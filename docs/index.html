    03 / FROZEN HOLDOUT AUDIT
    The scores don't agree on one winner.
    All 1,797 original test digits. Fixed k=10; seeds 100-109. Each arm chooses its lowest fitting inertia. No test-based model selection.
    PCA and whitening change distances. Do not rank raw inertia or silhouettes across geometries as if the metric were unchanged. ARI/NMI compare assignments with digit classes; they do not make this a classifier. NMI is not adjusted for chance.
    

    04 / OPTIMIZATION WITNESSES
    Descent is not global optimality.
    A six-point counterexample
    For [0, 1, 4, 5, 9, 10] and k=2, starting at [0, 1] reaches inertia 26.5. Exhausting all 31 nonempty binary partitions gives a better global value: 17.5. Monotone convergence alone is no certificate.
    

    What's actually implemented
    Manual NumPy Lloyd assignment/mean updates, D² k-means++ initialization, explicit empty-center repair, 10 full restart histories per arm, fitting-only sign-fixed SVD, arithmetic NMI, chance-adjusted Rand agreement, and all-row Euclidean silhouettes. Independent small references check objectives, scores and the local-minimum witness. No scikit-learn, PyTorch or remote inference.
    The unmodified UCI training and test files keep their official split. All 3,823 training features fit the four prespecified arms; labels are used only for external test scores and composition. Fixed configurations are written before test access. These descriptive results cover one dataset and these seeds, not a general advantage for seeding or whitening. No new clustering algorithm, class-discovery guarantee or production digit recognition is claimed.
