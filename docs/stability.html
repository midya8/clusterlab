What stability cannot tell you
Same-data restarts only measure sensitivity to these saved initializations. Stable arbitrary groups need not match digit classes. ARI weights pairs of observations and can hide disagreement in small clusters. Center matching ignores cluster populations and can disagree with the partition comparison. We never compare center movement across raw, PCA and whitened feature metrics, select k from this report, or replace the original minimum-fitting-inertia winners.
The v0.3 ambiguity audit adds best-distinct permutations, all selected-edge exclusions, exact optimal-permutation counts and strict sufficient entrywise-cost perturbation bounds. These preserve every previous matching witness. A zero reduced-cost edge alone does not prove an alternate optimum. See the source method for the finite-penalty proof and scope. The assignment solver is the established Hungarian shortest-augmenting-path method, implemented with Python's unbounded integers. Every binary64 cost is converted to its exact power-of-two ratio. All 220 witnesses are checked against an independent subset dynamic program; tiny randomized instances are also exhaustively enumerated. Rounded costs and duals are exported as decimal strings, not lossy JavaScript numbers.
All original fitting artifacts, test scores, holdout split and earlier demo remain unchanged. The only new measurements are comparisons between saved fitting partitions and centers. Original UCI data: Alpaydin & Kaynak (1998), CC BY 4.0. MIT code; established algorithms, no endorsement.
